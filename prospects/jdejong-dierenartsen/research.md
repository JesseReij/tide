## Sales notes

- **Who decides:** because Evidensia owns the practice, marketing may be partly central. Ask for the practice manager first, and ask if they have a local marketing budget or need approval from Evidensia NL.
- **Upside:** if it works here, the same format can go to other Evidensia practices in the region.
- **Risk:** Evidensia may have brand rules for logos and colours. Our pitch must clearly come from Tide and must not look like an official Evidensia piece.
