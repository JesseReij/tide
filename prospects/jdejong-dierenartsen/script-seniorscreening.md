# Script: Seniorscreening reel

- **Client:** J. de Jong Dierenartsen (pitch demo, made by Tide)
- **Format:** 1080x1920 (9:16), Instagram / Facebook reel
- **Length:** 20 seconds
- **Sound:** made to work without sound (most people scroll with sound off). Optional: soft music bed.
- **Copy rule:** we use only facts from their own website. We do not invent what the screening includes.

| Tijd | Beeld | Tekst in beeld |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 0.0 - 3.5 s | A dog's head, drawn in simple shapes, slowly turns grey around the snout. | **Uw maatje wordt ouder.** |
| 3.5 - 7.5 s | A heart line (like an ECG) runs across the screen, then a small magnifying glass moves along it. | **Niet alles zie je aan de buitenkant.** |
| 7.5 - 12.0 s | Three soft cards slide in, one by one: a heart, a paw, a speech bubble. | **Seniorscreening:** meer inzicht in de gezondheid van uw huisdier. |
| 12.0 - 16.0 s | The old price "EUR 199" is crossed out with one stroke. "EUR 139" grows in. A label says "tijdelijk". | **Nu tijdelijk EUR 139** (normaal EUR 199) |
| 16.0 - 20.0 s | The dog from the start comes back, happy, with a wagging tail. Name and button appear. | **J. de Jong Dierenartsen** / **Maak een afspraak** / 072 - 53 316 06 |

Before publishing for real: confirm the price and the end date of the promo with the practice.
