# Script: Orthopedie (informative animation)

- **Client:** J. de Jong Dierenartsen (pitch demo, made by TIDE)
- **Format:** 1080x1350 (4:5), Instagram / Facebook feed and website
- **Length:** 24 seconds, works without sound
- **Copy rule:** only facts from their homepage: their own X-ray equipment, plates and screws of high-grade steel, "from a small limp to a complex fracture", orthopaedics as their speciality for decades. No medical promises about recovery time.

| Tijd | Beeld | Tekst in beeld |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 0 - 4 s | A dog in side view walks across the screen and keeps lifting one back leg. | **Loopt uw hond mank?** |
| 4 - 9 s | The screen turns into an X-ray view. A bone lights up, then a crack appears in it. | **Eerst kijken we naar binnen.** / Met onze eigen röntgen. |
| 9 - 15 s | A steel plate slides onto the bone. Four screws turn into place one by one. | **Een plaat en schroeven van hoogwaardig staal.** |
| 15 - 19 s | The crack fades. Back to the dog, which now runs freely. | **Van een kleine kreupelheid tot een complexe breuk.** |
| 19 - 24 s | End card with name, button and phone number. | **Orthopedie is al tientallen jaren onze specialiteit.** / J. de Jong Dierenartsen / Maak een afspraak / 072 - 53 316 06 |

Before publishing for real: let the vet check the bone and plate drawing and the wording.
