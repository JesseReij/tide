## What they offer

- Specialisms: algemene, sport, manuele, geriatrie, schouder, hand, revalidatie na nieuwe knie of heup, long en COPD, oncologische zorg.
- Specialising since 2002. All therapists are in the Centraal Kwaliteitsregister.
- Partners: Beweegzorg Noordwest (networks Artrose, Schouder, Sport, Hand en Pols, Vitaliteit), ParkinsonNet, Chronisch ZorgNet. **Close cooperation with the orthopaedic surgeons and surgeons of Noordwest Ziekenhuisgroep and Medische Kliniek Velsen.**
- News: Valpreventiecursus, Gecombineerde leefstijlinterventie (GLI-programma).
- Client survey by Mediquest on the homepage.

## What is missing (our opening)

- No video or animation on the site. Specialisms are text plus a stock icon.
- Their strongest story (working with the hospital's orthopaedic surgeons on knee and hip rehab) is hidden in one paragraph.
- The GLI programme is covered by the basic insurance with a GP referral, but the site does not say so in a way people see.

## GLI facts we use (with sources)

- GLI is in the basic insurance since 2019 and is reimbursed with a referral from the GP, for people with overweight and extra risk factors or obesity.
  - https://www.cohesie.org/voor-patienten/overige-patienteninformatie/leefstijl
  - https://www.unive.nl/zorgverzekering/vergoedingen/gecombineerde-leefstijlinterventie
  - https://www.bnnvara.nl/kassa/artikelen/wat-wordt-er-vergoed-bij-een-gli-programma
- Sources disagree about the deductible (eigen risico). **We do not mention it.**
- Check with the practice which GLI programme they run and who can join.
