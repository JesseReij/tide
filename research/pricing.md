# What animation costs in the Netherlands (research, October 2026)

Reusable for all prospects. All numbers come from search-result snippets of the sources below. The pages themselves were blocked in our session, so **recheck the key numbers in a browser before we quote them publicly**. Most sources are studio blogs. Prices exclude BTW.

## 60 to 90 second explainer (2D / motion graphics)

| Source | Price | Year |
|--------|-------|------|
| Animation Agency | EUR 2,500 to 7,500 for 60 s. Motion graphics about EUR 2,500 to 5,000. Market range EUR 3,000 to 10,000 per minute. | 2025/2026 |
| Bigfish | 2D, 60 to 90 s: EUR 6,000 to 12,000 | 2026 |
| Searchlab | Typical 60 to 90 s 2D with custom illustration: EUR 3,000 to 5,000 | 2026 |
| Donker Media | EUR 2,995 per 60 s plus EUR 550 for the script | 2026 |
| Animatiestudio Fris | EUR 2,450 (basic) to EUR 2,950 (complete) for 60 s | n/a |
| Storytelling Studios | Motion graphics EUR 1,450 for the first minute, custom animation EUR 3,950 | n/a |
| Mediamax | EUR 1,250 for 30 s, EUR 2,000 for 1 min, EUR 3,500 for 2 min | 2026 |

**Short version for the pitch:** a 1-minute animation from a Dutch studio usually costs **EUR 2,500 to 7,500**, and premium studios charge up to EUR 12,000.

## Short social animations (10 to 30 s)

- Fris: 3 x 15 s for EUR 2,950.
- Mediamax: EUR 1,250 for 30 s.
- Animation Agency: a 30 s video costs 60 to 80% of the price of a 60 s video.

## Turnaround

- Usually **3 to 6 weeks** (Animation Agency, Clarify, Fris, Donker Media).

## What drives the cost

- Script, storyboard, illustration, animation, voice-over, music and revision rounds. Bigfish says only about one third of the budget goes to the animation itself. The rest goes to thinking, writing, design and sound.

## Evidence that animation helps (healthcare)

- **JMIR 2024 systematic review:** in 11 of 15 randomised trials (2,454 patients), patients remembered health information significantly better after an animation video than with usual care.
- **Frontiers in Digital Health 2022 review:** animations improve patient knowledge in the short term. The long-term evidence is limited.
- We found **no** studies specific to vets, physiotherapists or schools. Do not claim numbers for those sectors.
